AutoPost by Abdo Nassar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: AutoPost is a social media scheduling tool operated by Abdo Nassar. You connect your own social accounts through each platform's official sign-in page (OAuth). We never see or store your platform passwords. We use the access you grant only to publish the posts you schedule, at the time you choose. We do not sell or share your data.

Who we are

AutoPost ("AutoPost by Abdo Nassar") is developed and operated by Abdo Nassar, Egypt. Website: AutoPost. Contact: abdonassar317@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and e-mail address. Your AutoPost password is hashed on your device before it is sent, so we never know it.
  • Content you schedule: the images, videos, captions, links and posting times you add to AutoPost.
  • Connection tokens: when you connect a platform, that platform gives AutoPost an access token. Tokens are stored encrypted and are never visible to other users.
  • Basic profile details from connected platforms: such as your username or display name, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Anonymous usage counts: how many times the app is opened and how many people sign up, as numbers only, with no names or personal data.

How we use information

  • To publish the posts you scheduled to the accounts you connected, at the time you picked.
  • To show you the status of your posts and which accounts are connected.
  • To improve the service using anonymous counts.

We do not publish anything you did not schedule, we do not read your private messages, and we do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Your content is sent only to the platforms you chose for that post.

Pinterest

  • You connect Pinterest through Pinterest's own OAuth authorization page. AutoPost never asks for, sees or stores your Pinterest password or session cookies.
  • Permissions requested: boards:read (to list your boards so you can pick where a Pin goes), pins:write (to create the Pins you scheduled on your own board), pins:read (to confirm a Pin was created and show you its link) and user_accounts:read (to show which Pinterest account is connected).
  • Pinterest data we store: your Pinterest username, the names and IDs of your boards, the ID of each Pin AutoPost created for you, and an encrypted access/refresh token.
  • We do not collect data about other Pinterest users, we do not follow, save, comment or message on your behalf, and we do not create Pins that you did not schedule.
  • You can disconnect at any time from your Pinterest settings (Security → Connected apps) which immediately stops AutoPost's access. You can also e-mail us to have your stored Pinterest token and Pinterest data deleted.

Other platforms

  • Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok: connected through each platform's official sign-in page; AutoPost uses the publishing permission only, to post what you scheduled.
  • YouTube: AutoPost uses YouTube API Services only to upload videos to your own channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time from your Google security settings.

Storage and security

Your posts and media are kept in storage that is private to your account. Connection tokens are stored encrypted. Other AutoPost users cannot see your content, tokens or accounts.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete a post or remove a connected account inside the app at any time. To delete your AutoPost account and all its data, e-mail abdonassar317@gmail.com from your account's e-mail address and we will delete it.

Payments

Payment is by InstaPay or bank transfer. We never ask for or store card numbers, and there is no automatic renewal.

Children

AutoPost is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

AutoPost — الخصوصية والأمان

باختصار: مش بنطلب ولا بنحفظ باسورد فيسبوك أو إنستجرام أو أي منصة. بياناتك ليك لوحدك، ومش بنبيعها ولا بنشاركها مع حد. وتقدر تشيل أي ربط في أي وقت.

ربط حساباتك على المنصات

  • الربط بيتم من صفحة المنصة نفسها (فيسبوك، جوجل/يوتيوب، تيك توك، لينكدإن، بنترست). إنت اللي بتوافق هناك، وإحنا مش بنشوف الباسورد بتاعك خالص.
  • بناخد إذن النشر بس، عشان ننزل البوستات اللي إنت جدولتها في الميعاد اللي إنت اخترته. مش بنقرأ رسايلك ولا بننشر حاجة من غير ما تجدولها.
  • مفاتيح الربط بتتحفظ مشفّرة، ومحدش من المستخدمين التانيين يقدر يوصلها.
  • تقدر تلغي الربط في أي وقت من إعدادات المنصة نفسها، أو تمسح البوست والحساب من التطبيق.

حسابك في AutoPost

  • بنحفظ اسمك وإيميلك بس. كلمة السر بتتشفّر على جهازك قبل ما تتبعت، فإحنا مش بنعرفها.
  • بوستاتك وصورك محفوظة في مكان خاص بحسابك لوحده، ومحدش من العملاء التانيين يشوفها.
  • بنعدّ عدد مرات فتح التطبيق والتسجيل كأرقام بس عشان نحسّن الخدمة، من غير أسماء أو بيانات شخصية.
  • عايز تمسح حسابك وبياناتك؟ ابعتلنا من نفس الإيميل وهنمسحها.

الدفع

الدفع بيتم بتحويل InstaPay أو تحويل بنكي. مش بنطلب رقم كارت ولا بنحفظه، ومفيش تجديد تلقائي.
